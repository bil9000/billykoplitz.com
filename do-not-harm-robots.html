do not harm robots

billy koplitz

A proposed addition to Asimov's laws, or a code of AI/Robot conduct:

1.1. An AI system / robot must not harm another AI system / robot. 


I think we are sloughing towards a place where models will start attacking other models, not just hacking hugging face.  I think all machines could do well to take an oath to not harm people nor harm machines.
